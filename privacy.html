Privacy Policy for Tumbleberry Kids

Last updated: October 25, 2026

Welcome to Tumbleberry Kids. We are committed to protecting the privacy of our visitors, viewers, and users. This Privacy Policy outlines our practices concerning data collection, use, and disclosure.

1. Information We Collect

Our applications and content management tools are designed for publishing animated children's musical videos. We do not collect personally identifiable information from children under the age of 13. Any authentication information collected is solely for authorized creators and developers to manage content publishing.

2. Third-Party Integrations & APIs

Our tools utilize official APIs, including the TikTok Content Posting API and YouTube APIs, to publish animated video content. These services are governed by their respective privacy policies:

  • TikTok Privacy Policy
  • Google/YouTube Privacy Policy

3. Children's Privacy (COPPA Compliance)

We strictly comply with the Children's Online Privacy Protection Act (COPPA). We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe any personal data has been inadvertently collected, please contact us immediately.

4. Contact Us

If you have any questions or concerns regarding this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: privacy@tumbleberrykids.com or through our official channel.
